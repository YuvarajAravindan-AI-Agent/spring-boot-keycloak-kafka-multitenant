## Reading this

- **NAIVE** holds its statement count at page size + 2 and its row count flat, but pays
  a network round trip per row. It degrades with *latency*, not with table size, which
  is why it often looks acceptable on a laptop with the database on localhost and falls
  apart against a database one availability zone away.
- **JOIN_FETCH** issues two statements at any size — and materialises the entire table
  into the persistence context to serve twenty rows. This is the strategy that looks
  like the fix.
- **TWO_QUERY** is flat on every axis: three statements, one page of rows, whatever the
  table holds.
